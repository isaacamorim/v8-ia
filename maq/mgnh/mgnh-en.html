    Densimetric Tables

    Description

    The densimetric tables have the basic function of separating the incoming product composed of materials of different densities (such as stone debris, damaged, poorly formed, rough, cuttle and half-grain), in low, medium and high density fractions.

    The basic principle involves the flow of dry material on a tilted vibrating platform covered by screen. The constant airflow keeps the material in stratified flotation. Thus, the lighter material remains in the upper strata as it flows to the underside of the vibratory platform. Heavier material, such as stones and other materials, rises to the higher side of the inclined vibratory platform.

    The Novo Horizonte densimetric tables are placed in different configurations of grain processing systems, mainly before the packaging. They have a wide range of application for granular materials.
