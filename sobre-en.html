HISTORY

For more than 38 years in Brazilians' life.

The trajectory of Novo Horizonte originates in a small house, of precarious structure and less than 30 m², where the brothers João and Silvio Drago decided to start a company of supplies for grain processing machines.

It was not long before the small company, based on the honesty and quality of its products, conquered several partners, making its rise a natural consequence.

Since 1981 in the city of Limeira - SP, Novo Horizonte has achieved its market share through its core values, offering its customers not only equipment but integrated, socially responsible and innovative solutions capable of contributing to their partners And society.

Today, this recognition makes Novo Horizonte the leader in bean processing machines. In view of this and aware of its role in the life of Brazilians, the company was structured, has an industrial park of 7500 m² and a staff of 70 employees, expanded, has a strong presence in Latin America, and is ready to meet the national demand and International.

FIELDS OF WORK

Novo Horizonte operates in two areas:

Grain processing:

Area that consecrated the company in the market, grain processing is a tradition in Novo Horizonte and has more than 35 years of expertise. It provides complete solutions for the entire post-harvest chain, especially for beans, developing together with the client the project to be implemented, optimizing the process and detecting opportunities.

The equipment in this area consists of polishers, stone separators, gravity tables, cleaning and pre-cleaning machines, bean machines (classification set), sieve classifiers, silos, mechanical belt conveyors, horizontal continuous conveyors ), among others.

Packaging of products:

A vertiginous growth area in the company, the Novo Horizonte packaging and baling machines have great acceptance in the market, both nationally and internationally, offering innovative solutions for a highly competitive value. They are known for the quality of their materials, ease of handling and high productivity. The company has been active in this segment for more than 20 years.
