ABOUT US

Novo Horizonte has been exercising its role as vanguard in the supplying of solutions for the most diverse challenges in the field of cereal grain processing and packaging.


ORIGIN

Founded in 1981, the Indústria de Máquinas Agrícolas Novo Horizonte Ltda. is localized strategically in the city of Limeira, São Paulo. The company benefits from 100% of national capital.

PRESENCE

Our values ​​have been present for over 30 years in the market, a fact that currently grants us leadership in the segment of machines for the processing of beans.

AMBIENT

Novo Horizonte has an industrial park of 7500 m², where all our equipment is developed by experienced and qualified professionals, ready to meet all the requirements that the market offers.

PRODUCTS

We count on a select group of equipment, all designed and tested to ensure the quality and productivity expected of a Novo Horizonte equipment. We have two main areas of activity. The first one, in the grain beneficiation area, especially beans, has machines to service its entire post-harvest production chain. The second, in the field of packaging products, offers a modern line of machines for the packaging of grains, farinaceous, etc. Both areas benefit from our expertise and tradition.
